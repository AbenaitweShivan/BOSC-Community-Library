# Sustainability Strategy for BOSC Community Library

## Overview
This document outlines the long-term funding and sustainability
strategy for the BOSC Community Library, comparing two major
open source funding models and recommending the best fit.

## Model 1: Red Hat Service-Based Model
The Red Hat model offers free software but charges for professional
services such as support, training, and consulting.

### Advantages
- Generates consistent revenue through service contracts
- Keeps the core software free and open
- Attracts enterprise and government clients who need support
- Proven model used successfully by Red Hat and Canonical

### Disadvantages
- Requires a dedicated support team
- Revenue depends on client acquisition
- May shift focus from community to commercial clients

## Model 2: Foundation-Driven Model
The Foundation model relies on donations, grants, and sponsorships
managed by a non-profit foundation, similar to the Linux Foundation
or Mozilla Foundation.

### Advantages
- Community governed and community focused
- Eligible for government and academic grants
- Transparent financial management
- Builds long term trust with public sector institutions

### Disadvantages
- Revenue can be unpredictable
- Requires strong fundraising and grant writing skills
- Growth depends on donor generosity

## Recommendation
For the BOSC Community Library, the **Foundation-driven model**
is the better fit. As a public-sector focused library serving
educational institutions and government agencies, a foundation
model aligns better with our mission of transparency, community
governance, and public service.

We recommend pursuing the following funding streams:
- Government education grants
- University partnerships and sponsorships
- Corporate sponsorships from open source friendly companies
- Community donations through platforms like Open Collective

## Long Term Vision
The BOSC Community Library aims to become a self-sustaining
open source project governed by a diverse community of educators,
developers, and public sector professionals across Africa and beyond.
